# Before You Bid

Paste a freelance job post, get a one-screen verdict: apply, think twice, or skip - with the reasons.

## The Unique Kernel
It finds the two things that silently kill freelance applications: **hidden deal-breakers** (mandatory calls or daily stand-ups, recruiter middlemen, unpaid test work, a budget below your own rate) and **the question the client hides at the end of the post** ("To apply, tell us how you would...") that most applicants never answer.

## Who It's For
A solo freelancer who works in writing (no calls) and has a limited number of applications per month - e.g. 10 free applications on freelancermap, or Connects on Upwork. Today they read every post in full, often miss the line about weekly calls, and send a generic letter that ignores the client's question.

## The Core Loop
Open the page, paste a job post, read the verdict, copy the "answer this first" question into the proposal. Repeat for the next post.

## Inspiration & Identity
Calm, factual, like a checklist from a colleague who has read hundreds of posts. Each finding quotes the exact sentence from the post that triggered it - no black box.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
On 15.10.2026 the GL Tools agent drafted two applications; one project required a "regelmaessiger Jour-Fixe" (regular meetings) that the owner does not do, and the other ended with a question that the first draft never answered. Both mistakes are mechanical and should be caught before writing a single word.

## What "Working" Looks Like
A static page in the browser. Paste a real post (German or English) -> within a second it shows a verdict colour, the deal-breakers with the quoted trigger sentence, matching skills from the user's own skill list, and the client's question highlighted. The "oh, that's cool" beat: the question buried at the very end of a long post appears at the top as "Answer this first".

## The POC Boundary
- One page, runs offline, no account, no server, no API key.
- Rules for: meetings/calls, recruiters/agencies, unpaid test work, budget vs. own hourly rate, remote/on-site, application question.
- User's skills and minimum rate stored in the browser.
- German and English posts.

## Later
Browser extension that runs on freelancermap/Upwork pages; LLM-written proposal outline.

## Explicitly Cut
- LLM calls - the point is a transparent, instant, free check; quoting the trigger sentence matters more than eloquence.
- Scraping job boards - legal/ToS risk and not needed to prove the kernel.
